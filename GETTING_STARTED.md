# Getting started with The Counting

The Counting keeps track of the shows you watch. Everything stays on your own computer: no account, no cloud, no ads.

## Step 1: get a free TMDB key (about five minutes)

The Counting uses TMDB for show names, episode lists and artwork, and each person needs their own free key.

1. Create a free account at themoviedb.org.
2. Open your account Settings, then **API**, and request a key for personal use. Use "The Counting" as the app name and "personal, non-commercial" as the purpose. For the website field you can enter `http://localhost`.
3. Copy the **API Key**. You will paste it into The Counting the first time you open it.

## Step 2: open The Counting

### Windows

1. Unzip `TheCounting-windows.zip`. Keep the whole **The Counting** folder together.
2. Open the folder and double-click **The Counting.exe**.
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This appears once because the app is not signed.

### Mac

1. Check your Mac's chip: Apple menu, **About This Mac**. If it says **Chip: Apple M...**, use `TheCounting-mac-apple-silicon.zip`. If it says **Processor: Intel**, use `TheCounting-mac-intel.zip`.
2. Double-click the zip to unpack it, and drag **The Counting** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Double-click The Counting. Your Mac will say it cannot open it. Click **Done**.
4. Open **System Settings**, then **Privacy & Security**, and scroll down to **Security**. Click **Open Anyway** next to The Counting (it appears for about an hour after step 3), and enter your Mac password.
5. Open The Counting again and click **Open Anyway** once more. From now on it opens normally.

## Step 3: use it

Paste your TMDB key on the first screen, add a show, and tap **Mark watched** as you go. The Counting saves a backup copy of your list on its own.

The round button at the top right switches between light and dark. It follows your device until you choose (Profile, then Settings, then Appearance).

If anything goes wrong, open **Profile, then Settings, then Help** and use **Open The Counting's data folder**. The file `thecounting.log` there shows what happened.
